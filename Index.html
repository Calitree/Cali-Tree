How it works
A logical progression system

Each skill has prerequisites. You can't jump to a muscle-up without building the foundation. That's the point.

01
Prerequisite-gated skills
Every skill requires mastering its foundation first. The tree enforces the correct order — no shortcuts.
02
Honest goal tracking
Log reps or hold times per session. Time goals require a single unbroken hold — no adding up partial attempts.
03
AI coaching
A Groq-powered coach reads your actual progress and gives specific, actionable guidance — not generic advice.
04
Tutorial videos
Every skill has a curated tutorial. Watch inline or open YouTube. Step-by-step coaching cues for correct form.
05
Cloud sync
Progress syncs to Firebase under your username. Pick up where you left off on any device.
06
Legendary milestones
Muscle-up, front lever, pistol squat, planche — marked as legendary. Real benchmarks for serious athletes.
